LEARNING METHOD / 01

A visible system for steady progress.

Our framework joins clear goals, active recall, deliberate practice, feedback and realistic review—without promising shortcuts or universal results.

FOUR WORKING PRINCIPLES

Plan for evidence, not just activity.

  1. 01

    Define an outcome

    Replace “study chapter three” with something observable: explain the process, solve five varied problems, or compare two arguments.

  2. 02

    Retrieve before rereading

    Try to reconstruct the idea from memory, then use the source to correct and complete your model.

  3. 03

    Practise decisions

    Choose tasks that require the same judgment the real assessment, project or application will require.

  4. 04

    Review the process

    Record the error pattern, useful strategy and next question instead of judging the session by time alone.

RESOURCE ARCHIVE

Externalise the work.

A simple card, error log or weekly map reduces the load on memory and makes the next step easier to see.
